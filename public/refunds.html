This policy applies to one-time plan purchases, upgrades and add-ons (such as the Print kit) from Adpulse Nexus Digital Marketing Ltd. ("FinalChapter").

1. What you are buying

A one-time purchase unlocks the FinalChapter desk for your account: an organizer, checklist and letter drafts, and links to official sources. FinalChapter does not file any forms and is not legal, tax or financial advice. Please read What you get before buying.

The Print kit add-on is a digital PDF you download and print yourself. Nothing is mailed.

2. All sales are final

Because the desk (or the Print kit download) is unlocked right after payment, all purchases, including upgrades and add-ons, are final and non-refundable, except in the cases listed in section 3.

3. When we will refund you

  • Duplicate charges: if you were charged more than once for the same purchase, we will refund the extra charge.
  • Billing errors: if you were charged the wrong amount, or charged without completing a purchase, we will refund the amount charged in error.

4. How to ask

Email elisa@adpulsenexus.com from the address on your account, with the date of purchase. We aim to reply within 3 to 5 business days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Card issuers usually take 5–10 business days to show the refund.

When a payment is refunded, or disputed with your card issuer, the plan, upgrade or add-on it paid for stops working. Your estate information is not deleted; it is kept under our retention policy (3 years after your last activity).

6. Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under Ontario’s Consumer Protection Act, 2002 or other law that cannot be waived.
