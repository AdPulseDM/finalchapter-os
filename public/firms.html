FinalChapter for firms

For professional trustees, trust companies and estate consulting firms in Ontario

Who it’s for

Teams whose staff administer estates for a fee and want one consistent Ontario runbook across many client estates: corporate and professional estate trustees, estate consulting and settlement firms, and accounting or advisory practices that manage the executor work for clients.

What’s included

  • The Ontario step checklist with estimated CRA and Ontario deadlines for every estate
  • Letter drafts you can copy or download as Word or PDF
  • Cost tracker and estate administration tax estimate
  • Family & advisers list with a revocable view-only progress link for families
  • Client list with estate switcher and archiving
  • Staff seats and estate limits set by your plan and confirmed in your quote

What it isn’t

  • It doesn’t file anything. FinalChapter does not file, submit, sign or deliver forms, returns or applications to the court, the CRA or anyone else.
  • It isn’t legal or tax advice, and it is not a replacement for your lawyer’s court-filing or practice-management software.
  • Deadlines are estimates from official sources. Your staff still verify and act.
