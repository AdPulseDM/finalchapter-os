2. Do assets pass outside the estate?

Joint accounts or a home held as joint tenants usually pass to the survivor. RRSPs, RRIFs, TFSAs, pensions and life insurance with a named beneficiary go straight to that person.

3. Real estate in Ontario in their sole name?

Include property held as a tenant in common. Not property held as joint tenants with someone who survived them.

4. Is a bank or other institution asking for a certificate?

Only what passes through the estate: assets in their own name at death (Ontario real estate less its mortgage, bank and investment accounts without a beneficiary, vehicles, belongings). Do not subtract other debts, funeral costs or fees. What to include.
