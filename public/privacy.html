Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Adpulse Nexus Digital Marketing Ltd. ("FinalChapter", "we", "us"), operating at https://finalchapter-os.netlify.app, collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information. We handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and its fair information principles.

1. Who is responsible

Adpulse Nexus Digital Marketing Ltd., 537 Wilson Heights Blvd #1009, North York, ON M3H 2V7, Canada, is responsible for personal information under its control. Our privacy contact is the Adpulse Nexus head office, info@adpulsenexus.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, chosen plan, and a password. We store only a salted, one-way hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation that a payment was made, the plan or add-on bought (for example the Print kit), the amount, the Stripe session identifier and your email. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Desk content you enter: for example a date of death, an estimated estate value, task status, letter fields (names, account references, notes), estate or client names, cost entries, and Circle entries (names, relationships and roles of family members and counsel). This may include personal information about the deceased and about other people. Only enter what you need, and do not enter Social Insurance Numbers or full account numbers.
  • Documents you download: when you download a letter (.docx or PDF) or the Print kit, our server builds the file from the desk content already saved for your estate and sends it straight to you. We do not keep a copy of the file. The free probate check and tax calculator run only in your browser; what you type there is not sent to us or saved.
  • Technical information: our host may process IP addresses and request logs to operate and secure the site. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

We use your browser’s session storage to keep a random sign-in token (fc.token) so you stay signed in during a visit, the identifier of the estate you last opened (fc.estate), and, while you are paying for an upgrade, the plan you chose (fc.upgrade). Session storage is cleared when you log out or close the tab. If you tick “Keep me signed in on this device” when you log in, the sign-in token is kept in your browser’s local storage instead, so you stay signed in after closing the tab, until the sign-in expires (up to 7 days) or you log out; logging out removes it. Leave this unticked on a shared computer. Our site code does not set cookies, and it uses local storage only for that optional sign-in token. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so your browser sends Google a request, including your IP address, when the page loads. When you pay, you are sent to Stripe’s hosted checkout page, which is governed by Stripe’s own privacy and cookie policies.

3. Why we use it

  • to create and secure your account and let you log in;
  • to process your payment and unlock the plan you bought;
  • to save and show your desk content back to you;
  • to respond to support requests and to send service messages about your account or purchase;
  • to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

We do not sell personal information. We do not send marketing emails. We send only service and transaction messages, such as messages about your account, your purchase, or changes to these policies. If we ever offer marketing emails, they will be opt-in only, will include a way to unsubscribe, and will comply with Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

4. Consent

By creating an account you consent to the collection, use and disclosure described here. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits, by contacting us; withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the service. If you enter information about other people, you confirm you are allowed to do so, for example as the estate trustee.

5. Sharing, service providers and storage outside Canada

Share links you create. On the Family and Professional plans you can create a read-only progress link for an estate. Anyone who has that link can see the estate name, date of death, checklist progress and the names, relationships and roles in that estate’s Circle. They cannot see letters, estate values, costs or your account details. You can revoke the link at any time, and archiving an estate revokes it. Share the link only with people you trust.

  • Netlify hosts the website and runs the server functions and data storage (Netlify Blobs). See Netlify’s privacy policy.
  • Stripe processes payments. See Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) serves the web fonts used on our pages. Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load. See Google’s privacy policy.

These providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. Account, sign-in and desk data are stored with Netlify, Inc. in Netlify Blobs. Our code uses Netlify’s default region for site-wide stores, which Netlify documents as us-east-2, US East (Ohio), with data encrypted at rest and in transit (Netlify Blobs documentation). Netlify may cache data at edge locations and process requests in other locations. Payment data is processed by Stripe. Information stored outside Canada may be accessible to courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of that country.

6. Retention

We keep account and estate (desk) information for 3 years after your last activity (for example, your last sign-in or save), and then delete it automatically. You can ask us to delete it sooner by writing to info@adpulsenexus.com. Payment and transaction records are kept for as long as required by law (Canadian tax law generally requires six years), and then deleted.

7. Safeguards

We use reasonable safeguards for the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, expiring sign-in sessions and restricted administrative access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal information, or ask us to delete your account, by writing to info@adpulsenexus.com. We will respond within 30 days, or tell you if we need more time as PIPEDA allows.

9. Breaches

If a breach of security safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by PIPEDA.

10. Complaints

Contact the Adpulse Nexus head office at info@adpulsenexus.com first. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date. For material changes, we will also give notice by email to account holders where possible.
