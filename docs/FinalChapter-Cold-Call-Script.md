# Final Chapter — Cold-Call Script (Ontario estate / probate prospects)

**For:** Fiverr freelancers dialing on behalf of Adpulse Nexus Digital Marketing Ltd.
**Start:** Wednesday, 10:00 AM Toronto time (ET).
**Your job:** open, qualify, book. The owner closes. Do not negotiate price, discounts or firm terms.
**Time cap:** 6 minutes per call, unless the prospect asks for a live walkthrough.
**Recording:** do not record unless the owner has given you a written consent process to follow. See the handout.

---

## 1. Opener (about 20 seconds)

> "Hi, is this [Name]? This is [Your name], calling for Final Chapter. It's an Ontario executor organizer. I'll be brief. Is now a bad time for a 2-minute question?"

- **If it's a bad time:** "No problem. When should I call back?" Log the callback time in `notes`.
- **Firm or professional (trustee, estate consultant, paralegal, law office staff):**
  > "We help desks that handle Ontario estates keep the 180-day Estate Information Return, the creditor notice and the CRA deadlines in one checklist for each estate."
- **Individual executor (family member):**
  > "We help Ontario executors see every step, deadline and official form in one checklist, so they know what is due and when."

## 2. Discovery: three questions

Ask all three, then listen. Write short answers in `notes`.

1. **Role:** "Are you handling an estate yourself, or do you administer estates for clients?" *(This tells you if they are retail or a firm.)*
2. **Current method:** "How do you track the steps and deadlines now, for example the 180-day Estate Information Return, the final T1 and the TX19 clearance?" *(Listen for: spreadsheet, memory, a lawyer, government websites.)*
3. **Pain and volume:** "What is the hardest part to keep on top of? And roughly how many estates are you working on right now?" *(A firm with more than 5 estates is a firm lead. One estate is retail.)*

## 3. Pitch in one breath

> "Final Chapter is an organizer for Ontario estates. It gives you a 60-step timeline, the CRA and court deadline windows, letter drafts you edit, an Estate Administration Tax estimate, and links to the official forms like 74A, TX19, T1 and T3. It sits between the government websites and your lawyer. It doesn't file anything and it isn't legal or tax advice. It just keeps the whole estate in one place."

## 4. Soft close: book with the owner

> "Would a 15-minute walkthrough with our owner help? They can show you a sample estate and, for firms, put together a written quote. Does [day] at [time] work, or is another time better?"

- **Yes:** book through [CALENDLY_LINK]. Confirm their email. Set `booked = 1`.
- **"Show me now":** you may go past 6 minutes. Open the homepage *How it works* video (`/#how`) or the sample estate tour. Set `walkthrough_requested = 1`.
- **Maybe:** "Can I send the 60-second video link and call back on [day]?" Log the follow-up.
- **Wrap-up:** "Thanks, [Name]. You'll get a calendar invite from us. Have a good day."

**Retail vs firm**
- **Individual executor:** you may say "one payment, from $49 CAD" and point them to the plans on the homepage.
- **Firm:** say "firm plans are annual and quoted by the owner. You can see them after you create a free account at finalchapter-os.netlify.app/firms." **Never quote firm dollar amounts on a call.**

---

## 5. Objection replies

Keep each reply short. Acknowledge, reframe, then ask again.

**"The government sites are free."**
> "They are, and we link to them. We don't replace them. The hard part is that the information is spread over Ontario Court Forms, CRA and Service Canada pages. Final Chapter puts those steps in order, with the deadline dates for your estate. Would a quick look at the sample estate help you decide?"

**"I have a lawyer."**
> "Good. You should keep them. Final Chapter isn't legal advice and doesn't replace your lawyer. Executors use it to track their own tasks, such as letters to banks and utilities, the documents checklist and CRA dates, so the time they pay their lawyer for is spent on legal work. Would a 15-minute walkthrough be useful?"
> *Firm version:* "It's not filing software. It's a shared checklist so your staff aren't keeping the deadlines in their heads."

**"Too expensive."**
> "For one estate, it's a single payment from $49 CAD, with no subscription. For firms, the owner quotes based on how many estates and staff you have. Can I book a short call so you get exact numbers?"
> *(Do not offer discounts. Do not quote firm tiers.)*

**"Just send me an email."**
> "Sure. What's the best address? I'll send the 60-second video and the link. Can I also put a tentative 15-minute slot on your calendar? You can cancel it if the video answers your questions."
> Log the email in `notes` and set `interested = 1` only if they showed real interest. The owner or the approved template sends the email. Do not send pricing PDFs yourself unless the owner says so.

**"I'm not the executor."**
> "Thanks for telling me. Is there someone in the family, or at your office, who handles estate files? Do you know a good time to reach them?"
> If there is no one: thank them, set `not_interested = 1` and end the call. Do not push.

**Grief or a sensitive moment** *(not an objection, but it will happen)*
> "I'm sorry for your loss. I don't want to take your time today. Would it be all right if I sent a link you can look at later?"
> If they say no, end the call politely.

---

## 6. Never say

- "We file the forms for you" or "we submit to the court or the CRA." **False.**
- "You don't need a lawyer" or any legal, tax or financial advice. **Not our role.**
- "Upload your documents" or "secure vault." **There are no uploads. The documents tab is a have / not-yet checklist.**
- Firm dollar prices, discounts, or guarantees about deadlines or amounts.
- Anything that sounds like hype or pressure. Keep it plain and professional.
