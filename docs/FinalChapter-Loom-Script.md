# FinalChapter — Loom Script (How It Works, ~90 seconds)

**Presenter:** Elisa · **Site:** https://finalchapter-os.netlify.app · **Target length:** 75–90 seconds

> Tip: Have the site open in one tab with a sample estate ready. Speak at an easy pace — roughly 150 words per minute (about 210 spoken words total).

---

## 0:00–0:15 — Opener
**On screen:** Homepage hero at https://finalchapter-os.netlify.app. Slowly scroll past the headline.

**Say:**
"Hi, I'm Elisa from FinalChapter. Settling an estate in Ontario isn't one big task — it's dozens of small deadlines. FinalChapter is an Ontario runbook for executors. It's not a law-office suite, and it doesn't replace your lawyer."

## 0:15–0:35 — Sample estate → 60-step timeline + next deadline
**On screen:** Open the sample estate. Show the timeline view, scroll through a few steps, then point the cursor at the "next deadline" card.

**Say:**
"Here's a sample estate. Every estate gets a sixty-step Ontario timeline, from the first week through final distribution. Up top, you always see the next deadline — so the hundred-and-eighty-day return and the creditor notice stop living in someone's head."

## 0:35–0:50 — CRA / EAT calculator
**On screen:** Open the CRA windows / estate administration tax calculator. Type an example estate value and show the result.

**Say:**
"On the tax side, you'll see the CRA filing windows for this estate, plus an estate administration tax calculator. Enter the estate value and you get an estimate right away."

## 0:50–1:05 — Letter draft + documents checklist
**On screen:** Open a letter draft (e.g., a bank notification letter), show the pre-filled details. Then open the Documents checklist and mark a couple of items "Have it" / "Not yet" (nothing is uploaded).

**Say:**
"Next, letters. Pick one, like a bank notification, and it's drafted with the estate's details filled in. You review and send. Then the documents checklist — the will, the death certificate, account details — mark each one have it or not yet, so you know what's still missing. FinalChapter doesn't store the files; originals stay with you."

## 1:05–1:20 — Multi-estate / Professional or firm path
**On screen:** Show the estate switcher or dashboard with multiple estates. Then scroll to the pricing section and the firms section (#firms).

**Say:**
"Handling more than one estate? Professional covers up to five client estates for a one-time fee. And for trustee and consulting firms, we offer annual firm plans with staff logins, shared templates, and firm branding."

## 1:20–1:30 — Forms links + close + CTA
**On screen:** Open the forms section — show links for 74A, TX19, T1, T3, and the Estate Information Return. End on the contact info.

**Say:**
"Finally, we link the official forms — 74A, TX19, T1, T3, and the Estate Information Return. We organize; you or your counsel file. Want a written quote? Email info@adpulsenexus.com. Thanks!"
