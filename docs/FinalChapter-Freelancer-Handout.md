## Your role
- **You:** open, qualify and book. **The owner:** closes, quotes and handles pricing questions beyond the public retail prices.
- **6-minute rule:** keep each call to 6 minutes or less. The only exception is a prospect who asks for a live walkthrough.

## What we offer
- A **60-step Ontario estate timeline**, covering the probate check, the Estate Information Return (due 180 days after the certificate), the notice to creditors, and the final distribution.
- **CRA deadline windows** (final T1, T3, TX19 clearance) and a next-deadline dashboard.
- **Letter drafts** the user edits: bank, utility, landlord, insurer, employer, notice to creditors and TX19 cover letter. Download as Word or PDF.
- An **Estate Administration Tax (EAT) estimate**, plus a free probate check at `/probate`.
- **Official form links** (74A, TX19, T1, T3, ISP1200), each attached to the step that needs it.
- A **documents checklist** where each item is marked have / not yet.
- **Positioning:** it sits between "the government sites are enough" and "I need a lawyer for everything." It organizes the steps and does not replace either one.

## What NOT to promise
- **No filing.** It does not file, submit or send any form, return or letter to the court, the CRA, Service Canada or anyone else.
- **No advice.** It is not legal, tax or financial advice, and it does not replace a lawyer or the government websites.
- **No vault or uploads.** The documents tab is a checklist only. Users cannot upload or store files.
- No guarantees about deadlines or amounts. They are estimates the user must check with the official source.
- No discounts or custom terms. Send all of those to the owner.

## Call recording consent (Canada)
In Canada, businesses must tell people and get their consent before recording calls (PIPEDA). **The default is to not record.** Only record if the owner has given you a written process for explicit consent from both parties, and you follow it word for word on that call. If you're unsure, don't record.
